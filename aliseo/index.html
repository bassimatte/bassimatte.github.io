01 — WORKFLOW

One current.
Four directions.

Aliseo turns the iPad and Digitakt into one connected workspace, keeping the practical steps close to the sound.

01

Prepare

Trim, normalize, fade, convert to mono, concatenate or chop samples before sending them to the instrument.

EDIT · SHAPE · CHOP
02

Transfer

Browse iPad Files and the Digitakt +Drive side by side. Transfer samples, folders and selections with drag and drop.

FILES · TRANSFER · ORGANISE
03

Record

Capture the Digitakt’s stereo USB audio directly on iPad, with input monitoring, level meters, signal triggering and pre-roll.

MONITOR · CAPTURE · 24 BIT
04

Protect

Back up a Digitakt II project together with its referenced samples, then restore the complete package when needed.

BACK UP · RESTORE · CARRY

02 — CLOSE TO THE SOUND

See what you are carrying.

Waveforms, spectrum, playback position, looping and dBFS metering make each sample visible before it moves. The same focused view continues through editing and recording.
